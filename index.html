04/25/2025 -


I ripped a piece of my drywall paint while removing an adhered cable cover. I'm going to try and fix it by first chipping away at the loose bits of paint that I pulled up, then covering that over with primer and spackle, then sanding away at the loose bits of spackle around the edge so that it blends, then paint with the paint I got color matched at Lowes Home Improvement store. I really hope it matches enough to not get into too much trouble with a security deposit or them having to repaint anything extensive.

I was reading over some of my old diary entries, because I keep all of my diary entries in a single LibreWriter document called "notesdoc.odt", I can go back and re read specific days very easily, and because it is all typed out, and I do a pretty good job of self editing, the information stays legible, unlike other forms of diaries I've had in the past, like hand writing. Here is a journal entry from a little over one month ago:

From March 26th, 2025:

◦ Yesterday I felt like having a mental break. I was driving down the highway, losing control. Traffic was so fucking bad I was attempting to bypass some of it by riding down the fast lane, but when it was a reasonable time to try and get into the lane I needed to get into when the highway splits from i-95 to 64, nobody would fucking let me in. No matter how much I pleaded with my turn signal and looking and trying to wave but nope nada nuh uh not gonna happen today buddy oh well sayonara and so I kept on going and missed the exit. I ended up having to go like 15 minutes out of the way because of the way i-95 just takes a mighty curve away from the city and into the westside of Richmond and beyond, well, on my way to where I need to go, I was bout 10 minutes away by back road when I get to a motherfucking detour, guess what, the detour doesn’t take me to where I need to go, I don’t know what the fucking happened there, but Google Maps didn’t know how to handle the detour and so it took me another 15 mintues out of the way and back on to motherfucking highway 64 into shitty ass terrible ass motherfucking traffic and when that happened I got very sad and threw my sunglasses into the floorboard (they broke) and then cried in silence while I drove home. I was having a pretty good drive before that, too, I was listening to the dead and it’s so hard to be sad when you are listening to the dead and I wanted to cry so I needed to listen to silence because thats how sad I am nowadays. I’m just too sad for music.
◦ I’m so very excited to leave this place, I just always want ot leave, why do I always want to leave why oh why oh why?
◦ I’m watching Baby Invasion tonight on the projector, maybe in the next fivem inutes or so.
◦ Work has been getting worse and worse everyday and I don’t know what to do other than just sit there and take it all in, cry a little inside, let my coworkers talk shit and whisper about me behind my back, all that. I know they do it, I can sense it. I have a feeling jena and zach were talking shit about me when I left today and I just don’t give a fuck other than the fact that I’m just so ready to leave.
◦ I want to buy a passport. I think I am going to try and get a passport once I’m back in North Carolina, I might also try and get a weed registration card or whatever the fuck before I leave Virginia. I miss North Carolina. I miss Appalachia. I’ll be back soon."

I found it interesting to relive a temper tantrum that I threw in the car after getting too frustrated with traffic and my job. I imagine that it was the type of babyish temper tantrum that causes people to make very poor decisions, much worse than breaking a pair of $20 sunglasses (they weren't even my favorite pair, I probably would've restrained from throwing my favorite pair, which is a $6 pair from www.amazon.com that look a lot like the ones Abbas Kiarostami wore in "Homework" (A neorealist "documentary" about the way schoolkids think about their lives/educational careers in Koker, Iran.)) And to that point, I'm a little more confident in my self control, but I still reacted in that way that I wish I hadn't. Normally, or at least in the past, I've been able to control my emotions quite well, typically this is also when I have more friends, and am more succesful with dating, but lately, I've felt so worked up over every little thing, that I think it's best if I reflect.

Not getting worked up over anything, even the worst of things that could happen, used to be one of my strongest characteristics, but I no longer have that sense of calm. Is it because I haven't had a profound connection with nature in a very long time. I need to seek nature. I do seek nature. I love nature.

I think moving away from Richmond and back into a much less populated area will be beneficial for this aspect about me that I once loved. I remember the first time I watched a Jimi Hendrix interview, it was one of the Dick Cavett interview clips on www.youtube.com, in which Jimi talks about not needing much, he's got his guitar, that's enough for him, he's got his music that he plays for folks and that seems like enough to him, and he seems happy, he seems chill, he seems content. I've not been content for a long time, and I'd like to get back to that... but how?

Perhaps tree bathing will help, maybe running naked through the woods would help, going swimming in natural bodies of water, all that type of stuff.
