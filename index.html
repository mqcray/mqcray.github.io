This is the first blog I will be posting to this website. I also want to have a succesful portfolio of all of my works such as essays, films, books, poems, anything and everything that I want. Welcome to macraybraxton.com. I will try to be here everyday.

I watched all of Caveh Zahedi's "The Show About The Show" and I loved it. It's the type of work that makes you want to be a director, or at least makes me want to be a director, that is, of course, I already wanted to be a director, but Caveh makes films that make me want to be a director just like him. I want to make movies like his. They have an uncommon Quality about them that i just like a lot. That Quality reminds me of the same quality that i find in a Jafar Panahi film, or like in Symbiopsychotaxiplasm and Symbiopsychotaxiplasm take 2 1/2 by William Greaves, there is a feeling I get that I just can't explain. Oh Laura Poitras also has that Quality about her films, and perhaps Errol Morris as well. They don't all have to be documentaries or neorealism either, I think a filmmaker like David Lynch also shares this Quality, maybe just on a different plane.

That different plane is what I aspire to reach one day myself as a filmmaker. Beyond that plane I believe is becoming one of the greats of all time, your Kubricks, your Spielbergs, your Scorseses, not many else who can claim what they can claim. Even Tarantino isn't on that plane, but moreso on the previous plane described.
